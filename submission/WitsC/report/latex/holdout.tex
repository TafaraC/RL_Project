The final frozen transfer evaluation uses all three ft09-trained seeds of each
algorithm's baseline, novelty and final memory stage, unchanged on cd82, tr87 and
vc33. There are five rollouts per checkpoint per game, with action-sampling seeds
100--104 and the same 1,000-action budget: 270 episodes across six stages.
We reduced rollouts from 20 to five for this deadline-limited transfer check,
retaining three independent training seeds. No held-out outcome changed the
model selection or parameters. This tests transfer from a single donor game,
not online adaptation or multi-game training.

All PPO stages and both improved DQN stages complete zero levels on all three
held-out games. DQN baseline seed 1 completes one tr87 level in one of five
rollouts (one of 15 pooled baseline rollouts on tr87); its other seeds complete
none. All agents fail to win any game and consume their full action budgets.
The isolated completion does not establish reliable generalisation. The
training-seed-level results and frozen protocol are provided in the evidence
folder. A same-game development success therefore offers little assurance of
transfer to a new puzzle under this protocol.
