# Report formatting provenance

The assignment requires the RLC 2026 template, author names/student emails and no cover page. We use `rlj.sty` version 2026.1, without modifying its fonts, margins or spacing, with the `preprint` option and omit `makeCover`.

Official instructions: https://rl-conference.cc/2026/submissionInstructions.html

Official template link: https://www.overleaf.com/read/pyndjmbtgsvj

The Overleaf source was not retrievable directly in this environment. The 2026.1 style distribution was retrieved from the mirrored author kit linked by https://www.murfy.ai/es/resources/latex-templates/rlc-26 (retrieved 27 October 2026). The style header attributes the 2026 adaptation to Marlos C. Machado and Philip Thomas. The supplied style is copied unchanged.

Student email convention is documented at https://www.wits.ac.za/mywits/student-email/ as studentnumber@students.wits.ac.za. Emails in the report apply that convention to the user-supplied student numbers.
